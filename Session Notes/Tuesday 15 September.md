## Designed robot base and intake
### Issues ran into
The frame where the intake is attached is lower than last season because pollen is smaller but it needs to be equally as far forward so we had an issue where the axles couldn't go over the wheels (because they would interfere) to a great extent which led us to using two shorter axles rather than one longer one.
This led to the issue that we couldn't attach a motor at the same hole in the channel as the axle for the intake because we needed the space in the channel for shaft collars to secure it because we didn't have space on the outside due to the wheels.
Moving from that we decided to do a chain-sprocket system with the intake-motor further back in the channel. This also allowed us to gear it for more speed on the intake. I *pray* it works.
We doubled the rubber bands which made the force really intense which bent our frame because we made a U-frame with the intake at the opening.
We reinforced the frame at it's corners.
We decided to tie off elastic bands instead of double looping them.
### Design
Two motor intake with short axles
Geared for speed
Loose elastic bands
U-Frame with Reinforced corners
### Plans for the future
Add second droidan intake wheel for transfer
Reinforce frame with cross bar
