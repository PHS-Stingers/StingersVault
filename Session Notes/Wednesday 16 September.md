## Designed flywheel structure and output
### Issues ran into
The attaching of the pillars to secure the flywheel took a bit of time because we were attaching U-channel to C-channel so we had to attach the mount and the channel separately.
The pillars were attached too far back so we had to move them forward.
We use 3 planetary gear boxes (3:1, 4:1, 5:1) to make the flywheel motor faster.
We attached the flywheel motor to one of the pillars with a piece that goes perpendicular to the pillar.
We attached an axle to the motor and ran it to the other side of the robot but because the motor was ahead of the pillar it was attached to, it was also ahead of the pillar on the other side to we attached a second, smaller pillar to hold the flywheel. We used shaft collars to hold the flywheel in place rather than spacers because we saw no sense in using spacers. We also used a shaft collar to hold the axle in the motor because the shaft collar embedded in the motor was not doing it well enough.
### Design
One motor geared for speed with ultraplanetary gearbox
flywheel with single axle running across the robot.
No space between flywheel wheels
Near middle of bot
